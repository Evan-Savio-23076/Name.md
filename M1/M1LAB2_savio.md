# THe Peanut Butter Sandwhich

## Assumptions
- The bread, the peanut butter jar, and knife are all present.

## Steps
- open peanut butter jar
- remove twist tie 
- open bag of brread 
- grab knife
- cut bread with knife 
- remove seal
- scoop peanut butter with knife
- spread the peanut butter on slice 1. bare side down
- drop knife
- put slice 2 on slice 1, peanut butter side facing up

## Done When
- peanut butter sandwhich was made
